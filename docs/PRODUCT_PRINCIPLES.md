# Customli POS product principles
Customli.io owns and develops Customli POS. Smash Brothers Burgers is Customer #1 and the reference tenant, not the product identity.

The primary user is a small-business or medium-enterprise owner who is not expected to be technical.

1. Plain language before technical language.
2. Sensible defaults before configuration screens.
3. Guided setup before documentation.
4. One obvious operational action wherever possible.
5. Back Office holds complexity; frontline screens stay fast and focused.
6. Business capabilities are configurable, never hard-coded to one customer.
7. Device roles are Register, Kitchen Display and Customer Display.
8. Hardware failures explain what the owner can do next.
9. Routine menu, staff, printer, tax, payment and reporting changes must not require a developer.
10. SBB-specific behavior is reference configuration, not a platform assumption.
